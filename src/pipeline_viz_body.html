Why matching almost failed: construction joints

Two elements meet at every wall-to-slab and wall-to-wall joint — their surfaces sit within millimetres of each other. A bare "nearest sample wins" rule lets sensor noise decide, sometimes crediting a wall that isn't built yet. Same point, same two candidate surfaces, two different rules.

Does the estimate track reality?

Count-weighted progress, estimated from the point cloud alone versus the manifest's ground truth, across all 16 stages. Error peaks mid-construction and closes to 2.0 percentage points once every element is actually built — the pattern SPEC.md's phase 8 flagged as the signature of a pipeline that's actually working, not one masking failures behind the uncertain-exclusion.
